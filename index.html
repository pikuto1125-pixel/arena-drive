Arena Drive

Arena Drive is a personal tool that copies the owner's own work files to the owner's own Google Drive and reads them back. It is used only by its owner and is not offered to anyone else.

Arena Drive は、持ち主本人の作業ファイルを本人の Google Drive に保管し、読み戻すための個人用ツールです。持ち主以外には提供していません。
